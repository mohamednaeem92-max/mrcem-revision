NCBI cranial-nerve references support the displayed answers. Facial palsy causes inability to close the eye, while eyelid elevation is supplied by CN III and sympathetic fibres; facial nerve taste and lacrimal parasympathetic functions are preserved concepts. Vagal palsy causes uvular deviation away from the weak side, hoarseness, dysphagia, and loss of gag, whereas tongue deviation toward the affected side indicates CN XII. Glossopharyngeal nerve supplies parotid secretomotor fibres and is the afferent gag limb. The optic nerve is the afferent limb of the pupillary light reflex. CN XI exits through the jugular foramen and supplies sternocleidomastoid and trapezius. CN VI supplies lateral rectus. A posterior communicating artery aneurysm can compress CN III and cause a fixed dilated pupil with down-and-out eye.
