## Batch rule

An option, answer-key, explanation, high-yield note, or mnemonic may be added or amended only after the original page establishes the record and a rank 1-3 external source supports the corresponding anatomy fact. A record remains an OCR draft unless its source validation is complete; ambiguous pages remain unresolved.
