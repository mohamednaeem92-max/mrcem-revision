| 1602 | Question 72 reads: “A 65 year old lady presents to ED complaining of sudden onset visual loss. On examination you demonstrate visual loss on the right side of the vertical midline with macular sparing in both eyes. Which of the following blood vessels is most likely occluded:”. Options: posterior communicating artery; posterior inferior cerebellar artery; posterior cerebral artery; anterior cerebral artery; middle cerebral artery. Green check is beside posterior cerebral artery. Explanation states the posterior cerebral artery supplies the occipital visual cortex and causes contralateral homonymous hemianopia with macular sparing. Displayed key is index 2. |
| 1603 | Question 73 reads: “A 65 year old lady, with a history of hypertension and diabetes mellitus, presents to ED with a 1 hour history of sudden onset of weakness and numbness solely in her left leg and foot.
