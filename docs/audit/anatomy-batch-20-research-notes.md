# Anatomy Batch 20 Research Notes

## Evidence-gate convention

The original scan controls the literal question, option order, and displayed key. An OCR draft is restored only when authoritative external evidence supports that source-keyed formulation at the required specificity. Exact anatomical-landmark or pathway claims that cannot meet this standard remain excluded from answer learning.

| Record | Source-keyed fact assessed | External evidence recovered | Preliminary disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| `p0624-q0019` | Primary uterine supply: uterine artery from internal iliac | NCBI identifies uterine arteries as the main vessels supplying the uterus and as branches of the internal iliac artery. [1] | Restore candidate |
| `p0625-q0020` | Pancreatic regions: epigastrium and left hypochondrium | NCBI lists pancreas in both epigastric and left hypochondriac regions. [2] | Restore candidate |
| `p0626-q0021` | Uterine visceral afferents: T10–L1 | A peer-reviewed pelvic-pain review describes T10–L1 visceral pain fibres innervating uterus, adnexa, and cervix, while also noting hypogastric axons reaching T10–L2. [3] | Restore candidate with variation note |
| `p0627-q0022` | Intraperitoneal pancreatic part: tail | NCBI classifies the pancreatic tail in the true abdominal cavity while listing pancreatic head and body as retroperitoneal. [2] | Restore candidate |
| `p0628-q0023` | Aorta posterior to third and fourth parts of duodenum | NCBI directly states only that the third part passes anterior to the aorta and IVC; it does not establish the source’s combined third-and-fourth formulation. [4] | Restrict |
| `p0629-q0024` | Ureteric entry landmark: pubic tubercle | NCBI confirms ureteric entry at the ureterovesical junction but does not support the exact pubic-tubercle surface landmark. The source key is therefore not restored. [5] | Restrict |
| `p0630-q0025` | Rectus sheath: three flat-wall-muscle aponeuroses | NCBI states that fascial coverings of external oblique, internal oblique, and transversus abdominis comprise the rectus sheath. [6] | Restore candidate |
| `p0631-q0026` | Renal arteries posterior to pancreas | NCBI confirms their aortic origin but the inspected authoritative text does not directly support the exact generic posterior-to-pancreas formulation. [7] | Restrict |
| `p0632-q0027` | Omental bursa posterior to stomach and liver | The Batch 17-reviewed NCBI lesser-sac reference supports its position behind the stomach and liver and anterior to the pancreas. [8] | Restore candidate |
| `p0633-q0028` | Infraduodenal common bile duct posterior to pancreatic head | NCBI states that the bile duct lies in a groove on the posterosuperior surface of the pancreatic head or is embedded in it before entering the descending duodenum. [9] | Restore candidate |
