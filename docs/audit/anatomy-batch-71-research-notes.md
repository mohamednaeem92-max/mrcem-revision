# Anatomy Batch 71 Research Notes

Batch 71 covers ten Central Nervous System records from `Anatomy-All.pdf`, source pages 1925–1945. All ten queued source records were inspected directly; intervening pages 1932–1944 were explanatory cranial-nerve learning sections with diagrams that were not required to answer the question records.

The spinal-cord tract references support question 71: the anterolateral/spinothalamic system carries pain and temperature, with a contralateral deficit beginning below the lesion. Source: [NCBI Bookshelf, Neuroanatomy, Spinothalamic Tract](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK545145/).

The posterior-cerebral-artery and visual-cortex references support questions 72, 75, and 76. PCA occlusion can produce contralateral homonymous hemianopia with macular sparing when the occipital visual cortex is involved; the primary visual cortex lies in the occipital lobe. Sources: [NCBI Bookshelf, Neuroanatomy, Posterior Cerebral Arteries](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532260/) and [NCBI Bookshelf, Visual Field Deficits](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK10912/).

The cerebral arterial and spinal-root references support questions 73 and 74. The anterior cerebral artery supplies the medial frontal/parietal cortex, including the paracentral lobule representing the lower limb; the C7 root is tested by the triceps reflex. Sources: [NCBI Bookshelf, Neuroanatomy, Anterior Cerebral Arteries](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK532259/) and [NCBI Bookshelf, Deep Tendon Reflexes](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK562238/).

The internal-capsule and Broca-area references support questions 64–65 in the source sequence: the internal capsule contains projection fibres, and Broca area in the dominant inferior frontal gyrus mediates expressive language. Sources: [NCBI Bookshelf, Neuroanatomy, Internal Capsule](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK542181/) and [NCBI Bookshelf, Neuroanatomy, Broca Area](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK526096/).

The cranial-nerve references support questions 1–4 in the continuation: CN IV palsy causes impaired downward gaze and vertical diplopia; the corneal reflex uses V1 afferently and CN VII efferently; diabetes can cause pupil-sparing ischemic CN III palsy; and CN IX is the afferent limb of the gag reflex. Sources: [NCBI Bookshelf, Trochlear Nerve](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537173/), [NCBI Bookshelf, Facial Nerve](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK526119/), [NCBI Bookshelf, Oculomotor Nerve](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK537126/), and [NCBI Bookshelf, Glossopharyngeal Nerve](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK539877/).

No question in this batch requires an image to determine its displayed answer. The explanatory images in pages 1932–1938 and 1942–1943 will remain documented as learning content only. All restored records will remain unapproved OCR drafts and mock-excluded.
