| 1611 | Question 2 reads: “A 64 year old lady complains of a sore right eye. On examination, you note a normal corneal blink reflex on her left side but no consensual blink reflex on her right side. A lesion in which of the following structures is most likely to cause this clinical picture:”. Options: ophthalmic nerve; oculomotor nerve; optic nerve; facial nerve; trigeminal nerve. Green check is beside facial nerve; red X is beside optic nerve. Explanation states V1 is afferent and facial nerve is the efferent pathway, with loss of the right consensual blink suggesting right efferent loss. Displayed key is index 3. |
| 1617 | Question 3 reads: “Oculomotor (CN III) palsy with sparing of the pupillary reflex is most likely caused by which of the following:”. Options: aneurysm of the posterior communicating artery; brainstem metastases; diabetes mellitus; cavernous sinus disease; subdural haematoma. Green check is beside diabetes mellitus. Explanation contrasts compressive peripheral parasympathetic-fibre injury with ischaemic central-fibre injury in diabetes. Displayed key is index 2. |

| 1621 | Question 4 reads: “Which of the following clinical features would you most expect to see in a glossopharyngeal nerve palsy:”. Options: tongue fasciculations; uvular deviation; loss of jaw jerk; loss of gag reflex; hoarseness. Green check is beside loss of gag reflex; red X is beside uvular deviation. Explanation states glossopharyngeal nerve is the afferent pathway of the gag reflex, while hoarseness and uvular deviation may occur in vagus palsy. Displayed key is index 3. |

| 1629 | Question 6 reads: “A patient complains of headaches and dizziness. Imaging reveals a tumour at the cerebellopontine angle. | 1634 | Question 7 reads: “Regarding the abducens nerve, which of the following statements is CORRECT:”. Options: abducens nerve innervates inferior oblique; abducens nerve palsy results in vertical diplopia; abducens nerve originates in medulla; abducens nerve enters orbit through inferior orbital fissure; abducens nerve palsy results in a convergent squint. Green check is beside the fifth option; red X is beside the second. Explanation states the nerve originates in pons, enters via superior orbital fissure, innervates lateral rectus, and palsy causes convergent squint and horizontal diplopia. Displayed key is index 4. |
